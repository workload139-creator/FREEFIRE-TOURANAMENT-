Clash Squad system

  • 1v1: 2 players · 2v2: 4 players · 3v3: 6 players · 4v4: 8 players.
  • Har format ka apna registration, player count, room access aur leaderboard hai.
  • Entry fee ₹10 per player. Admin share ₹8 per completed tournament.
  • Winner prize: 1v1 = 20 Diamonds, 2v2 = 38 Diamonds, 3v3 = 56 Diamonds, 4v4 = 84 Diamonds. Admin winner ko prize distribute karega.
  • Har approved kill par 2 Coins. Kill submission ka same process: registered UID match, gameplay/history video aur 2 screenshots; AI assessment ke baad admin final review.
  • Admin room ID/password eligible players ko private bhejega. Player ko 5 minutes ke andar YES confirm karna hoga.
  • Tournament LIVE hone se pehle eligible player entry cancel karke refund le sakta hai.
  • Wallet/payment BR ke saath common rahega; CS registrations aur leaderboards alag rahenge.
